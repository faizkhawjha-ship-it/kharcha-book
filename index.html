Privacy Policy for Kharcha Book

Last updated: October 2026

Kharcha Book does not collect, store on servers, transmit or share any personal data. All transactions you enter are stored locally on your device only. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

If you use the Export CSV feature, the file is shared only through the app or service you choose.

Deleting the app deletes all its data from your device.
